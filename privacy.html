Privacy Policy

Fairway U · Last updated November 25, 2026
Support

This policy explains what information Fairway U ("the app") handles. The short version: we collect nothing about you ourselves. Apart from purchases, which Apple handles, the app sends data off your device only through Google's advertising software, which shows the one ad in the game. Buying Remove Ads turns that off.

Information we collect

None. Fairway U has no accounts, no sign-in and no servers of its own. Your careers and settings are stored on your device, and in your device backups if you use them. Deleting the app deletes them from your device.

Advertising

The free version shows one full-screen ad before each new season, served by Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). The app requests non-personalized ads only. It does not ask for permission to track you across other companies' apps and websites, so it does not have access to your Apple advertising identifier.

To serve and measure ads, prevent fraud and fix problems, Google's SDK may collect information such as:

  • Device and app information (device model, operating system version, app version)
  • IP address, which can be used to estimate your general location
  • An identifier for the app or its developer on your device
  • App and ad interactions (for example, app launches, or whether an ad was shown or tapped)
  • Crash and performance diagnostics

Google collects and processes this data under its own policies. See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

If you buy Remove Ads, the app stops requesting and showing ads, and from the next time you open it Google's SDK is not started.

In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. The app never sees your payment details. It only receives confirmation from Apple that a purchase was made.

Links

The Credits screen links to websites, including OpenStreetMap and this one. They open only if you tap them, and each site's own policy applies. This site is hosted by GitHub, which may log visits under its privacy statement.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about privacy: sevenredbry@gmail.com. If you email us, we use your address only to reply.
