What was changed

Holes, greens, fairways, tees, bunkers, water and trees came from OpenStreetMap and were converted for the game. Courses were given fictional names. Some holes were lengthened, reordered or re-parred, trees were added or cleared, and pins, terrain and difficulty were added.

Elevation courtesy of the U.S. Geological Survey, 3D Elevation Program.
